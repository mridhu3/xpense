# XPense — 3-Minute Hackathon Demo Video Script

**Format:** Screen recording with voice-over

**Target duration:** Approximately 3 minutes

**Suggested title:** *XPense: Make Every Rupee Count*

## 0:00–0:15 — The problem

**Visual:** Show a phone screen with several small expenses appearing throughout the day, followed by a confusing end-of-month balance.

**Narration:**

> Most people do not lose control of their money because of one huge purchase. It happens through dozens of small decisions: food, travel, shopping, subscriptions, and impulse spending. Traditional expense trackers make users do all the work, and by the time they understand their spending, the money is already gone.

> We built XPense to make personal finance simple, intelligent, and actionable.

## 0:15–0:35 — The product

**Visual:** Open the XPense home screen. Show the greeting, available-to-spend balance, monthly budget, spending progress, and smart nudge.

**Narration:**

> XPense is a privacy-first, AI-assisted budgeting app designed around one question: “How much can I safely spend right now?”

> Instead of overwhelming users with spreadsheets, the home screen turns their financial data into a live money snapshot: available balance, monthly budget usage, forecasts, category spending, and practical nudges.

## 0:35–1:00 — Quick expense capture

**Visual:** Navigate to Add Expense. Demonstrate a normal manual entry with amount, category, wallet, and save. Then show the receipt scan and voice quick-add options.

**Narration:**

> Logging an expense takes seconds. Users can enter it manually, scan a receipt, or speak naturally.

> With receipt scanning, XPense uses AI vision to extract the merchant, amount, date, and likely category from an uploaded image. With voice quick-add, a user can say, “I spent 280 rupees on dinner,” and XPense converts that sentence into an editable expense.

> The user stays in control: extracted information can be reviewed before it is saved.

## 1:00–1:25 — Budget intelligence

**Visual:** Return to Home. Change the monthly budget in Settings. Add an expense that pushes spending beyond the budget and show the warning popup and negative available balance.

**Narration:**

> XPense makes the budget real, not theoretical. Users can customize their monthly limit, switch between months, and see exactly how much remains.

> If spending exceeds the budget, XPense does not hide the problem. Available money becomes negative and the app explains the over-budget state with immediate feedback. This creates awareness at the moment a decision is made.

## 1:25–1:50 — Goals and motivation

**Visual:** Open Goals. Add a savings goal, contribute a custom amount, reduce a contribution, and delete a goal.

**Narration:**

> Budgeting is not only about cutting spending. XPense helps users build toward something meaningful with flexible goal vaults.

> Users can create multiple savings goals, choose any contribution amount, add more savings over time, reduce an earlier contribution, or remove a goal. The experience is flexible because real financial plans change.

## 1:50–2:15 — Gamification and AI Coach

**Visual:** Open Quests. Scroll through quests, XP progress, streaks, and badges. Return to Home and tap Ask my coach.

**Narration:**

> To make good habits stick, XPense adds gentle motivation through quests, XP, levels, streaks, coins, and badges. The progress bar shows users exactly how close they are to the next level.

> The AI Coach then turns activity into advice. It analyzes the selected month’s spending and savings goals to provide practical suggestions—not generic financial quotes, but specific next actions.

## 2:15–2:35 — Personalization, privacy, and sync

**Visual:** Open Profile. Change the profile name and avatar. Show the Firebase account card and Settings notification controls.

**Narration:**

> XPense also adapts to the user. Profiles support a custom name and avatar, while email and password authentication allows users to access their account across devices.

> The app is local-first and works offline. When cloud sync is enabled, Firebase securely separates each user’s data. Push notifications, UPI intent payments, camera receipt capture, and microphone input extend the app beyond basic expense tracking.

## 2:35–3:00 — Closing and impact

**Visual:** Fast montage of the dashboard, receipt scan, goal vault, AI Coach, and APK installation screen. End on the XPense logo.

**Narration:**

> XPense combines fast capture, AI assistance, budget accountability, savings goals, and habit-building in one focused experience.

> Our goal is not to make people think about money all day. It is to give them the clarity to make better decisions in the few seconds when it matters.

> XPense: less financial guesswork, more intentional spending. Thank you.

## Recording notes

- Record at a phone-sized viewport so the interface is easy to follow.
- Keep screen transitions quick; each feature should take no more than 20–25 seconds.
- Use a calm, confident voice and pause briefly after each product benefit.
- Use a sample account with clearly fictional data. Do not show real email addresses, Firebase credentials, tokens, or personal transactions.
- For the AI Coach and receipt scan, prepare a successful result in advance or record the flow with a stable network connection.
- Export the final video at 1080p, with captions enabled if the hackathon platform supports them.
